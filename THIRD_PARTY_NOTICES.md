## Legacy MOV.AI Warehouse model

`src/my_first_bot/models/Warehouse` identifies Filipe Almeida / MOV.AI as its
author in `model.config`. The downloaded model does not contain a license file,
and its exact upstream asset license has not yet been confirmed.

The model is used only by the legacy `newwarehouse.world` and
`fuel_warehouse.world` profiles. It is not used by `real_warehouse.world`, the
main simulation profile. Keep this repository private and do not redistribute
that legacy asset publicly until its original license has been verified or the
asset has been replaced.
